### multi-016 | multiturn
U: What's the weather usually like in Darwin?
S: Darwin is tropical. There's a wet season from about November to April, hot, humid and stormy, and a dry season from May to October, which is sunny and warm.
U: When's the best time to visit?
S: Most people go in the dry season, especially June to August. Clear skies, lower humidity and the outdoor markets are pumping.
U: And the wet season?
S: The wet has its own charm: dramatic lightning storms and waterfalls at full flow in places like Kakadu. But some roads close because of flooding, so plan carefully if you go then.
