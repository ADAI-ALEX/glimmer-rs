S: A leap year has an extra day, the twenty-ninth of February, added every four years. It keeps our calendar lined up with the Earth's trip around the Sun, which takes about three hundred and sixty-five and a quarter days.

### gen-037 | general
U: What's the closest star to Earth?

### gen-038 | general
U: How do bees make honey?
S: Bees collect nectar from flowers and store it in their honey stomach, where enzymes start breaking it down. Back at the hive, they pass it between bees and fan it with their wings to evaporate water, until it thickens into honey.

### gen-039 | general
U: What's the difference between a virus and bacteria?
S: Bacteria are tiny living cells that can survive on their own, and many are actually helpful. Viruses are much smaller and can only reproduce inside other cells. Antibiotics work on bacteria but not on viruses.

### gen-040 | general
U: Why do we have time zones?
S: Because the Earth spins, different places face the Sun at different times. Time zones keep noon roughly when the Sun is highest wherever you are, instead of everyone using one clock.
