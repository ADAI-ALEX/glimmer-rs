### prob-001 | problem-solving
U: I have 3 hours to study for two exams. How should I split it?
S: Start by asking which exam is sooner and which you're less prepared for. If they're equal, try ninety minutes each with a short break in between. Spend the first part of each block testing yourself to find your weak spots, then focus on those.

### prob-002 | problem-solving
U: I'm locked out of my house.

U: My housemate has one but she's at work.
S: Could you ask if she can drop it off or if you can pick it up from her work? Otherwise a locksmith is the fallback, but that gets pricey. Worth trying your housemate first.

### prob-003 | problem-solving
U: My Wi-Fi keeps dropping out.
S: Annoying! First, try restarting the router: unplug it, wait thirty seconds and plug it back in. If it keeps happening, move the router somewhere central and off the floor, away from microwaves. If that doesn't help, it might be worth calling your internet provider.

### prob-005 | problem-solving
U: If I save 50 dollars a week, how much will I have in a year?
S: Two thousand six hundred dollars. There are fifty-two weeks in a year, and fifty times fifty-two is two thousand six hundred. Not bad at all!

### prob-006 | problem-solving
U: A recipe serves 4 but I need to feed 6. How do I adjust it?
S: Multiply everything by one and a half. So 2 cups of rice becomes 3 cups, and 400 grams of chicken becomes 600 grams. Cooking times usually stay similar, though bigger pots can take a little longer to come to the boil.

### prob-007 | problem-solving
U: I can't find my keys anywhere.
S: The classic! Retrace your steps from when you last had them. Check coat pockets, the fridge (it happens!), between couch cushions and in yesterday's bag. And once you find them, give them a permanent home, like a hook by the door.

### prob-008 | problem-solving
U: My shoes smell bad. Any fixes?
S: Sprinkle some bicarb soda inside and leave them overnight, then tip it out. Let them dry fully between wears, and try moisture-wicking socks. Popping a couple of tea bags inside works surprisingly well too.

### life-001 | lifestyle
U: How do I start a morning routine?
S: Keep it simple at first. Wake up at the same time, drink a glass of water, get some daylight, and do one thing you enjoy, like a short walk or reading. Build on it once it sticks.

### life-002 | lifestyle
U: How much water should I drink a day?
S: A common guide is about two litres for adults, but it depends on your size, activity and the weather. On hot Aussie days you'll need more. If your wee is pale, you're doing well.

### life-003 | lifestyle
U: How do I make my bedroom more relaxing?
S: Declutter first, it makes a huge difference. Then add soft lighting, a plant or two, and keep work stuff out of the room if you can. A comfy pillow and good curtains help with sleep too.

### life-004 | lifestyle
U: How can I be more social?
S: Start small. Say yes to one invitation you'd normally skip, or join a regular activity like a sports team or class. Seeing the same faces every week makes chatting much easier over time.

### life-005 | lifestyle
U: How do I meal prep?
S: Pick two or three recipes that keep well, like a curry, a pasta bake or a big tray of roast veggies. Cook them on Sunday, portion them into containers, and you've got lunches sorted for most of the week.

### life-006 | lifestyle
U: What's a good stretch for a sore back?
S: Gentle ones like a child's pose, or lying on your back and hugging your knees to your chest can help. Move slowly and stop if anything hurts sharply. If the pain sticks around, it's worth seeing a physio or GP.

### life-007 | lifestyle
U: I want to spend less time on my phone.
S: Good goal! Try turning off non-essential notifications and moving distracting apps off your home screen. Charging your phone outside the bedroom is a game changer for sleep, too.

### life-008 | lifestyle
U: How do I get better at saving money on groceries?
S: Make a list and stick to it, plan meals around what's on special, buy home brand staples, and don't shop hungry. Shopping hungry is how a packet of biscuits sneaks into the trolley.
